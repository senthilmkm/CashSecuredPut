CashSecuredProfit

Privacy Policy

Effective Date: September 16, 2026

1. Overview

CashSecuredProfit is committed to protecting your privacy. We design our application to keep your financial data on your local device.

2. Data Storage & Local Persistence

All portfolio positions, trade parameters, and custom preferences entered in CashSecuredProfit are stored locally on your device using encrypted storage. We do not transmit or sell your trade logs to external marketing databases.

3. Market Data Requests

To fetch live stock quotes and put option chains, CashSecuredProfit sends anonymous ticker queries (e.g. AAPL) to secure brokerage data APIs (Public.com proxy). No personal identifying information is attached to market quote requests.

4. Subscription Purchases (RevenueCat & Apple iTunes)

In-App Subscriptions are processed securely by Apple iTunes App Store services and RevenueCat. CashSecuredProfit does not store or access your credit card details.

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions regarding this Privacy Policy, please contact our support team at support@cashsecuredprofit.com.
