How do I restore my Pro subscription?

Open CashSecuredProfit, navigate to Settings > Pro Subscription, and tap "Restore Existing Purchases".

What is included in the Free Tier vs Pro?

The Free Tier includes the Cash-Secured Put Calculator, Health Scorecard (0–100), and Strategy Guides. Free tier includes 1 saved trade and 1 saved wheel on this device, and shows your 3 most recent closed trades. Deleting them does not unlock another. Upgrading to Pro unlocks unlimited positions, full lifetime trade history, automated expiration alerts, live option chains with 1-tap auto-fill, Roll Down and Out execution, and PDF/CSV exports.

How does the 7-Day Free App Trial work?

Both annual and monthly Pro subscription plans include a 7-day risk-free trial. You can test all Pro features with zero charge. You can cancel anytime in your Apple ID Settings at least 24 hours before the trial ends without being charged.

How does the Roll Down & Out Simulator work?

When an existing put position moves against you or nears expiration, the Roll Simulator lets you simulate buying back your current contract and selling a new strike/expiration date. It automatically calculates the net credit or debit, freed collateral, and your updated cumulative effective cost basis.

How do I export assigned trades to CoveredProfit?

When a Put trade is assigned, tap "Assigned & Export" in your Portfolio tab. The app calculates your net cost basis (Strike - Premium) and gives you a 1-tap link to manage in the Wheel Suite tab, open CoveredProfit, or copy parameters to clipboard.
