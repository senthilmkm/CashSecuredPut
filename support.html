How do I restore my Pro subscription?

Open CashSecuredProfit, navigate to Settings > Pro Subscription, and tap "Restore Existing Purchases".

How does the 7-Day Free App Trial work?

When you install CashSecuredProfit, you automatically get 7 days of full Pro access. After 7 days, you can choose to subscribe to Pro or continue using the Free Tier (up to 3 active saved trades).

How do I export assigned trades to CoveredProfit?

When a Put trade is assigned, tap "Assigned & Export" in your Portfolio tab. The app calculates your net cost basis (Strike - Premium) and gives you a 1-tap link to open CoveredProfit or copy parameters to clipboard.
